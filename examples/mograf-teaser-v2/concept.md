# Concept — mograf teaser v2

Product truth to dramatize: **every mograf piece ships with what made it — prompt, skills, source, copy.** v1 *told* that (a terminal typing, then demo widgets). v2 must *show* it with one idea and a real camera.

## Direction A — "Anatomy" (exploded piece)  ← CHOSEN
**Concept:** A finished motion piece comes apart in 3D into the four layers it ships with — prompt, skills, source, copy — then slams back together into the mograf mark.
- **Signature moment (t = 2.70 s, 19% of runtime):** the poster frame we have been watching *explodes along z* into four glass-thin planes; the camera swings 30° around the stack so you see the piece's anatomy in depth, each plane carrying real material (the actual prompt line, the skill names, the HTML source, the X caption).
- **Why it stops the scroll:** a flat feed post suddenly has a back side. A designer instantly reads "exploded view" (Apple hardware grammar) applied to a *motion graphic* — the thing itself is being dissected. Curiosity: "what's on the layers?" forces a rewatch.
- **Rides:** rule `depth-scatter-assemble` (radial-explode variant, z = i × 170) + `3d-camera-flight` (one camera state, orbit leg + landing) + `depth-of-field-blur` (rack per layer) → blueprint `logo-assemble-lockup` (assemble-from-parts finisher). Breakdown move 13 *Exploded View*, move 11 *Camera on the Real Product* (the product is the piece), move 18 *Dark Room Light*.

## Direction B — "The Curve"
**Concept:** The whole film is one ease curve: a clay dot rides a bezier drawn in a graph editor, the camera starts following the same curve, every word arrives on it, and it finally flattens into the underline of the wordmark — "taste is a curve, and we ship ours."
- **Signature (≈ 5.5 s):** the dot leaves the graph-editor grid and the *frame itself* moves along the curve (the camera becomes the ease).
- **Why it stops:** motion designers recognise the graph editor instantly; seeing the curve *drive* the shot is a nerd-snipe.
- **Rides:** `svg-path-draw` + `viewport-change` + `kinetic-type-beats`; breakdown move 14 *Contact Chain*.
- **Why not:** clever for animators, opaque for the indie-hacker half of the audience; it sells *easing*, not *the library ships the recipe*.

## Direction C — "One prompt deep" (the long shot)
**Concept:** An infinite dive through the clay diamond — each diamond is a window into another library piece (kinetic type → 3D stack → map → chart), the camera keeps falling through windows-within-windows, then pulls back out to reveal they were all tiles on mograf.si.
- **Signature (≈ 5 s):** the first fall through the diamond, Droste-style, with the next piece already playing inside it.
- **Why it stops:** recursive zooms are hypnotic and loopable on X.
- **Rides:** blueprint `camera-journey` (B, cursorless flight) + `zoom-out-workspace-reveal`; breakdown move 4 *Technique Ledger*, move 7 *Fill-the-Frame Match*.
- **Why not:** it is a montage of demo pieces again (v1's weakness, "range" instead of an idea), needs 4 credible mini-pieces, and the recursion only pays off at 20 s+.

## Pick: A — "Anatomy"
One sentence: **A finished motion piece comes apart in 3D into the four things every mograf piece ships with, then snaps back into the mark.**

It is the only direction where the motion *is* the product claim (taking a piece apart = "you get the source"). It forces the craft bar naturally: real z-depth and an orbiting camera (item 3), rack focus as the way we read each layer, a physical slam (shake + impact) for the reassembly, and a single shader seam where the reassembled piece *becomes* the mark.

Rhythm: `still-SNAP-slow ORBIT-tick-tick-tick-tick-SLAM-warp-HOLD`.
