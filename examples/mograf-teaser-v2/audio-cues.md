# Audio cue sheet — mograf teaser v2

The public copy ships **silent**. The published render used the SFX below; the Pixabay files are not redistributed here (the Pixabay Content License does not permit redistributing the files as standalone files). The room tone was generated by us and is left out to keep the repo small.

To restore sound, source equivalent sounds under a license you are comfortable with (Pixabay, CC0 on freesound.org, your own recordings) and add `<audio>` clips inside `#stage` in `index.html` at these times, for example:

```html
<audio id="sfx-explode" src="sfx/whoosh.mp3" data-start="2.61" data-media-start="0" data-duration="0.57" data-volume="0.708" data-track-index="12"></audio>
```
