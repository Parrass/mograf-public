## Changes from the published render

- **Camera shake.** The original used the HyperFrames registry `camera-shake` component with its `handheld-tele-strong` profile. That profile's numbers were measured from Unity Cinemachine's noise presets, so we left it out of the public copy. The impact now uses `JOLT` in `index.html`: a 6-frame table of pixel offsets we tuned by eye. It reads very close to the original at 30 fps, but it is not identical.
- **Audio.** Removed (see above). The render on mograf.si has sound.
- **GSAP.** Loaded from `node_modules/` rather than a vendored copy.

No music. No third-party imagery. No logos other than mograf's own.
